# Product Vision — Compatibility Redirect

The former v1.0 Product Vision is superseded by the approved [Product Vision v3.0 — The Living-World Adventure](Product%20Vision%20v2.0%20%E2%80%94%20Hybrid%20Creature%20Exploration%20RPG.md).

Tiny Titans is a Zelda-inspired hybrid adventure with original ecological puzzles, exploration, secrets and compact regional challenges. Solo and co-op are equally supported. The canonical player character is the standard Roblox avatar; the Baby Bug custom player visual is retired.

The north star defines product identity and ambition. The [GDD v3.0](GDD%20v2.2%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Living%20World%20Adventure.md) defines the detailed adventure experience, [Gameplay Blueprint & MVP Contract](Gameplay%20Blueprint%20%26%20MVP%20Contract.md) defines the first playable product, and [Tiny Titans Master Roadmap](Tiny%20Titans%20Master%20Roadmap.md) is the sole execution roadmap.

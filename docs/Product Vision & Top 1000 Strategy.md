# Product Vision — Compatibility Redirect

The former v1.0 Product Vision is superseded by the approved [Product Vision v4.0 — A Replayable Zelda-Style Adventure](Product%20Vision%20v2.0%20%E2%80%94%20Hybrid%20Creature%20Exploration%20RPG.md).

Tiny Titans is an original Zelda-style Roblox action-adventure designed for solo play, co-op with friends and replayability. The game adopts the broad gameplay language of Zelda — exploration, traversal, combat, puzzles, tools, equipment, dungeons, bosses, NPCs, quests, secrets and progression — while using an original world, story, characters, art, items and puzzle expression.

The project is not constrained to a miniature ecosystem or small-creature fantasy. The standard Roblox avatar is the canonical player character. Baby Bug, Tank and Glider are historical prototypes, not playable classes.

The [GDD v4.0](GDD%20v2.2%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Living%20World%20Adventure.md) defines the detailed adventure experience, [Zelda Mechanics Adaptation Matrix](Zelda%20Mechanics%20Adaptation%20Matrix.md) inventories mechanic families, [Gameplay Blueprint & MVP Contract v4.0](Gameplay%20Blueprint%20%26%20MVP%20Contract.md) defines the first vertical slice, and [Tiny Titans Master Roadmap v4.0](Tiny%20Titans%20Master%20Roadmap.md) is the sole execution roadmap.

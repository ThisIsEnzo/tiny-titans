\# TINY TITANS

\## Game Design Document — Prototype v0.1



\*\*Platform:\*\* Roblox  

\*\*Genre:\*\* Evolution / Survival / Multiplayer  

\*\*Target prototype size:\*\* 1 small arena, 2 evolution branches  

\*\*Development budget:\*\* $0  

\*\*Purpose:\*\* Validate the core evolution gameplay loop.



\---



\## 1. High concept



Start as a tiny creature in a living ecosystem. Eat, grow, survive and evolve into a creature with a different playstyle.



The central promise:



\*\*You don't just become bigger. You become something different.\*\*



The prototype must demonstrate that choosing an evolution meaningfully changes how a player moves, survives and interacts with other players.



\## 2. Target player experience



The player should:

\- Understand the objective without a tutorial.

\- Immediately recognize food and growth opportunities.

\- Feel progression within the first few minutes.

\- Anticipate an evolution choice.

\- Notice a meaningful difference after evolving.

\- Want to try the alternative evolution.



\## 3. Core gameplay loop



1\. Spawn as a small creature.

2\. Explore the arena.

3\. Find and consume food.

4\. Gain evolution energy.

5\. Reach the evolution threshold.

6\. Choose one of two evolution branches.

7\. Experience a new ability.

8\. Continue exploring and interacting with other players.



The prototype does not require a long-term progression system.



\## 4. Evolution branches



\### Starting form: BABY BUG



\- Small creature.

\- Basic movement.

\- Can consume food.

\- No special ability.



\### Branch A: TANK



Fantasy: survive, defend and resist threats.



Prototype ability:

\- Temporary defensive shell.

\- Reduced incoming damage or knockback.

\- Visible activation feedback.

\- Cooldown.



\### Branch B: GLIDER



Fantasy: mobility and exploration.



Prototype ability:

\- Short glide or air movement boost.

\- Ability to reach otherwise inaccessible small platforms.

\- Visible activation feedback.

\- Cooldown.



The abilities must be mechanically different, not just visually different.



\## 5. Arena



One compact natural environment containing:

\- Open ground.

\- A few elevated surfaces.

\- Food spawn locations.

\- Safe exploration areas.

\- A small number of obstacles.

\- Clearly visible points of interest.



No large open world.



\## 6. Food and growth



Food objects:

\- Spawn in predefined locations.

\- Can be consumed by players.

\- Award evolution energy.

\- Provide immediate visual feedback.



Growth:

\- A visible progression indicator.

\- A simple evolution threshold.

\- Optional subtle size increase before evolution.



Growth must not make character collision or movement unpredictable.



\## 7. Multiplayer



The prototype should support multiple players in the same server.



Players should:

\- See each other's forms and evolution.

\- Be able to compete for food.

\- See ability activation.

\- Interact without requiring a complex combat system.



Combat is optional for the first playable version. If it threatens the schedule, remove it.



\## 8. UI



Minimum UI:

\- Evolution energy/progress.

\- Current creature name.

\- Evolution choice when available.

\- Ability cooldown.

\- Basic interaction prompts.



No shop, inventory, daily rewards or premium currency.



\## 9. Out of scope



\- 30+ creatures.

\- Multiple biomes.

\- Breeding.

\- Pets.

\- Trading.

\- Complex combat.

\- Permanent account progression.

\- Monetization.

\- Large social hub.



\## 10. Prototype success criteria



Internal experimental targets:

\- At least 80% of testers understand the objective without verbal explanation.

\- At least 70% reach an evolution.

\- At least 60% express a desire to replay.

\- Testers can explain a meaningful difference between TANK and GLIDER.

\- Players do not describe the experience as simply collecting food to increase a number.



These are our evaluation targets, not Roblox industry benchmarks.



\## 11. Main risks



1\. The initial growth loop may feel repetitive.

2\. Evolution choices may not be meaningful.

3\. The arena may feel empty.

4\. Players may prefer only the strongest branch.

5\. Character scale changes may cause technical problems.



\## 12. Prototype definition of done



A tester can:

\- Join the experience.

\- Move around.

\- Find and consume food.

\- Gain evolution energy.

\- Reach the evolution choice.

\- Select either branch.

\- Use the corresponding ability.

\- See other players and their selected forms.



The complete flow must work in a real Roblox Studio multiplayer test.
